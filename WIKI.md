# LegitBooster - Wiki & Module Guide

Welcome to the official LegitBooster Wiki! This page contains detailed information about every module, how they work, and how to configure them for maximum stealth and performance.

---

## 🛡️ Combat & Defense Modules

### **LegitAutoPot (Auto Soup)**
*Category: COMBAT*
Automatically consumes soup (or potions) when your health falls below a specific threshold. 
- **Configuration:** You can set the health threshold in the ModConfig.
- **Stealth Feature:** LegitBooster waits for a randomized human-like delay before dropping the empty bowl. It will also seamlessly replace the empty slot with a new soup from your inventory (Smart Slot Restoration).

### **LegitAutoClicker**
*Category: COMBAT*
Simulates human clicking patterns to give you a PvP advantage without triggering anti-cheats.
- **Jitter Mode:** Simulates hand shaking during intense PvP.
- **Whitelist:** Can be set to only activate when holding a Sword or an Axe.

### **AutoSprint**
*Category: MOVEMENT*
Automatically holds the sprint key for you, as long as your hunger allows it. Stops sprinting when sneaking or colliding.

## 🏗️ Building & Movement

### **JumpBridge / NinjaBridge**
*Category: MOVEMENT*
Helps you bridge faster across gaps. 
- **JumpBridge:** Instantly places blocks at the peak of your jump.
- **NinjaBridge:** Automatically sneaks at the edge of blocks and unsneaks when placing to mimic speed bridging.

### **InvMove**
*Category: MOVEMENT*
Allows you to walk, sprint, and jump even when you have your inventory, a chest, or a crafting table open. (Very useful for looting!).

---

## 💡 Visuals & Trolling

### **Fullbright**
*Category: RENDER*
Sets your in-game brightness/gamma to maximum, allowing you to see clearly in deep caves or at night without needing torches.

### **Derp**
*Category: FUN*
Sends spoofed rotation packets to the server. Your camera remains normal, but other players will see your character's head spinning wildly in all directions.
