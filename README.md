## 🌟 About The Project

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&pause=1000&color=FF00FF&center=true&vCenter=true&width=435&lines=Your+Secret+Weapon;Play+Like+A+Pro;100%25+Undetectable" alt="Motto" />
</div>

**LegitBooster** is an advanced, strictly *legit-focused* utility mod designed for Fabric. Our primary goal is to provide seamless Quality of Life (QoL) improvements, combat assistance, and heavy FPS optimizations **without** setting off anti-cheat alarms on major servers. 

Unlike blatant hacked clients, LegitBooster focuses on the *subtle* details: randomized delays, human-like movements, and inventory management that looks completely natural to server-side checks.

## ✨ Key Features

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&pause=1000&color=00FF00&center=true&vCenter=true&width=435&lines=Smart+Combat+Assist;Auto+Defense+and+Soup;FPS+Boosting+Engine;Legit+Auto+Bridging" alt="Features Typing SVG" />
</p>

- **⚔️ Advanced Combat Utilities:** Human-like Jitter clicking, AutoSoup with randomized drop delays, and more.
- **🚀 FPS Booster:** Integrated rendering tweaks and optimizations to maintain high framerates during intense fights.
- **🏗️ Smart Bridging:** NinjaBridge and JumpBridge features that perfectly mimic speed-bridging techniques.
- **🎒 Inventory Management:** Move seamlessly while your inventory is open.
- **🛡️ Unrivaled Stealth:** Built from the ground up to bypass modern anti-cheats (NoCheatPlus, Vulcan, GrimAC).

## 🚀 Installation Guide

Getting started with LegitBooster is incredibly simple! Follow these steps to install the mod:

1. **Download Fabric Loader:** Install the [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft **1.16.5**.
2. **Download Fabric API:** Download the [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (1.16.5) and place it in your `mods` folder.
3. **Download LegitBooster:** Head over to our [Releases Page](https://github.com/Abdullaherain0131/LegitBooster-MultiVersion/releases) and download the latest `.jar` file.
4. **Install the Mod:** Place the `legitbooster-1.16.5.jar` into your Minecraft `.minecraft/mods/` directory.
5. **Launch & Play:** Start your game using the Fabric profile.

## 🎮 How to Use & Configuration

LegitBooster is designed to be plug-and-play, but you can customize everything to fit your playstyle.

- **Open the Menu:** Press the `Right Shift` (RSHIFT) key to open the in-game LegitBooster Configuration Screen.
- **Toggle Modules:** You can easily turn modules ON or OFF using the sleek graphical interface.
- **Detailed Guides:** Want to know what a specific module does? Check out the **[📖 Official Wiki](WIKI.md)** for a complete breakdown of every feature, delay setting, and stealth configuration!
